# Acme Support Guide

## Password reset
To reset your password, select Forgot password on the sign-in page. Enter the email address associated with your account. We will send a secure reset link that expires after 30 minutes. Check your spam folder if the email does not arrive. For security, support agents cannot set or share your password.

## Refunds
You can request a refund within 30 days of purchase if you are not satisfied. Contact support with your order number and the email used at checkout. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Annual subscriptions are eligible for a prorated refund only where required by local law.

## Shipping
Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 5 business days after dispatch. You will receive a tracking link by email when your order ships. If tracking has not updated for more than 5 business days, contact our support team with your order number.

## Contact support
Our support team is available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Time. Email help@example.com and include your account email and a short description of the issue. Never send passwords or full payment card numbers.
